## Positioning

**Core promise:** real help instead of empty promises. Differentiators: free first consultation
with an upfront legal assessment; transparent fixed prices (flat-rate modules, no hidden costs);
**personal lawyer reachability via WhatsApp**; 100 % digital possible; specialisation on the
**self-employed**, a niche hardly served. Not another free counselling office, not an anonymous
law-firm website, but *legal tech + personal lawyer advice*.

Slogans used: "Raus aus der Schuldenfalle. Endlich wieder frei atmen." ("Out of the debt trap.
Breathe freely again.") and "Wir bauen die Infrastruktur für einen schuldenfreien Neustart."
("We are building the infrastructure for a debt-free fresh start.")

## Tone of voice

| Trait | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Empathetic | debtors are ashamed; meet them at eye level |
| Competent | legally correct, professionally sound |
| Clear | plain language, no legalese — "what does this mean *for you*?" |
| Direct | no empty phrases; honest assessment, even when uncomfortable |
| Empowering | "you are not your debt"; there is a way out |

Forbidden: unexplained jargon; fear-mongering ("if you don't act now…"); unrealistic promises
("debts gone in 24 h"); demeaning words ("debtor", "broke") — prefer "those affected"; prefer
"way out of debt" (a process) over "debt freedom" (a state); real numbers, concrete steps,
questions ("do you know what a P-account really protects?").

Visual identity: trust blue `#066aab`, clean white, dark anthracite accent; Inter / Plus Jakarta
Sans / Space Grotesk; professional, card-based, minimal; LegalService structured data.

## Personas

**"Thomas" — the overwhelmed self-employed (primary).** 35–55, 60/40 male/female, self-employed,
small business or freelancer, 1,500–3,500 EUR net (fluctuating), 15,000–80,000 EUR debt, Berlin,
medium-to-high digital skills.
Pain points — existential fear ("every morning, fear of the letterbox"; "the tax office wants
15,000 and I have no idea where from"), shame and isolation ("my partner doesn't know how bad it
is"; "as a self-employed person you don't fail — or do you?"), overwhelm ("I don't even understand
the court letters"), bad experiences ("the free counselling had 3 months waiting time"; "the last
lawyer wanted 2,000 EUR up front").
Searches — emergency ("account seizure what to do now", "bailiff coming tomorrow"), research
("private insolvency yes or no", "P-account allowance"), comparison ("free counselling vs lawyer").
Journey: trigger → Google search → first click (article or district page) → trust (guides,
calculator, FAQ) → book a free first call → first call → mandate (fixed-price module) → processing.
What convinces: free start; transparent prices; personal reachability on WhatsApp; "he understands
my situation"; speed (no 3-month wait); fully digital.

**"Sabine" — the worried partner (secondary).** 30–55, searches on behalf of a partner ("partner has
debts what to do", "am I liable too?").

**The partner lawyer (tertiary, B2B).** Lawyers of other fields, tax advisers, counselling offices
interested in referrals, tool use, cooperation — reached via LinkedIn and the platform.

Content implications: questions as headings; always a concrete next step; emotional validation
*before* legal information ("you are not alone"); Flesch reading ease above 60; district pages with
local context (local court, district office, counselling offices).

## SEO strategy (phase 1)

Principle: **people with debt problems google; they don't ask on LinkedIn.** Goal: page 1 for
every relevant query in Berlin.

Keyword clusters: (1) debt counselling Berlin (incl. "free", "appointment", "self-employed");
(2) insolvency counselling (consumer insolvency process, regular insolvency for the self-employed,
duration); (3) P-account and garnishment protection (allowance of the current year, garnishment
table — update yearly); (4) debt in general ("debts what to do", "out-of-court settlement",
"ignore collection?"); (5) one landing page per Berlin district (12), of which 5 existed.

Content types: guide articles 2–4/month; FAQ pages; district pages; calculator tools linked from
the blog; thought leadership on LinkedIn 1–2×/week; news on legal changes (e.g. the yearly
garnishment table).

YMYL ("your money or your life"): Google judges E-E-A-T more strictly here, so every generated text
must be legally correct (lawyer as review instance), refer to professional advice, show the lawyer
as author, and cite sources (§ references, judgements).

Targets set for 2026: organic traffic +50 % / +200 % (Q2 / Q4), keywords on page 1 10 / 30,
district pages 8 / 12, articles 10 / 30, bookings via SEO 5 / 20 per month.

Reported by March 2026: 9 articles live, 5 of 12 district pages, technical audit done (meta
descriptions for all pages, 39 alt texts, 248 images compressed, security headers, thin content
expanded, slugs optimised); 33 keywords mapped in 5 clusters, without volume data.

## Competition (anonymised categories)

The competitor analysis listed 19 competitors in five categories:

| Category | Count | Typical strength | Typical gap SchuldenPRO saw |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local law firms | 6 | content on consumer and regular insolvency, emotional positioning, multiple locations | no calculator, no digital tools, dated sites, debt only a side topic |
| Local commercial (non-lawyer) advisers | 3 | exact-match domains, ranking for "self-employed" | no lawyer, keyword stuffing, no real E-E-A-T |
| Free counselling offices (charities, associations) | 5 | strong brands and trust, niche focus on the self-employed in one case | waiting times of 2–4 months, weak websites, little content |
| Institutional / government pages | 3 | unbeatable authority for navigational queries | directories only, no content |
| National portals and directories | 2 | strong domains, national reach | impersonal, no lawyer, no tools |

Gaps identified: the self-employed; digital-first tools; real district pages; up-to-date garnishment
data; a practising lawyer as visible author and trust signal.

## Go-to-market for the platform

From the pitch: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook ads, Google SEO/SEA; free analysis as lead magnet; free
consultation as end of the lead process; conversion to the 50 EUR/month subscription. Partner lawyers
via LinkedIn and legal-tech networks. See [04](04-business-model.md).

The SEO content production itself was automated with an agent pipeline: [12](12-ai-agents.md).
