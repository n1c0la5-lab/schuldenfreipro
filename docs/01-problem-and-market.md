project's own estimates as cited there; the concept paper attributes the over-indebtedness
figures to the usual German credit-agency and counselling-association reports.*

|---|---|

The heavily-indebted-but-not-yet-over-indebted group was singled out as especially
relevant: early intervention there prevents the most social and economic damage.

## What exists today

| Segment | Size | Price for the debtor | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social debt counselling (charities, municipalities) | ~1,350–1,440 offices, 500,000–600,000 cases/year | free (public funding) | overloaded, regionally uneven, waiting times of 6+ months, help usually arrives after escalation; not scalable |
| Lawyers | ~95,000–100,000 consumer insolvencies per year | 1,000–2,500 EUR out of court; 1,500–3,500 EUR incl. insolvency | high quality but expensive and fragmented |
| Commercial debt advice | — | 1,500–4,000 EUR | often opaque, quality varies, unaffordable for most |

## Market size (estimate)

| Segment | Estimated annual revenue |
|---|---|
| Legal debt counselling | 250–400 million EUR |
| Commercial debt counselling | 300–500 million EUR |
| **Total** | **600–900 million EUR** |

The pitch rounded this to "a 900-million-euro market without a digital competitor".

## The insight that started the project

The founders came from practice: one of them had handled 500+ cases in social debt
counselling and around 40 cases in private (paid) counselling. Their central observation:

> The most laborious element of debt counselling is **file keeping and capturing the
> debts**. The technology in the industry is antiquated — no modern software, let alone
> automated processes.

The incumbent desktop tool of the industry (an Excel/VBA application) creates settlement
plans, fills the official insolvency forms and drives Word mail merges. Everything is
manual data entry. SchuldenPRO's bet was to make the process **document-driven and
AI-native** instead of form-driven: the documents the debtor already receives become the
data, and the lawyer only checks and signs.

## Why now (as argued in the pitch)

- No digital competitor in the market.
- A business model designed to stay within the Legal Services Act (see [03](03-legal-model-rdg.md)).
- Demand validated by the founders' own practice (40 paid precedent cases, 45,000 EUR revenue).
- Debt is counter-cyclical: in hard times demand rises; the structural need is independent
  of the business cycle.
